# Code of conduct

IDK Clips is built by and for creators. We want everyone who takes part (in issues,
pull requests, discussions and any other project space) to feel welcome and respected.

## Our standards

Do:

- be respectful and patient, especially with newcomers;
- give and accept constructive feedback about the work, not the person;
- assume good intent and ask before you conclude;
- credit other people's work.

Do not:

- harass, insult or demean anyone, publicly or privately;
- make unwelcome sexual comments or advances;
- discriminate based on age, body, disability, ethnicity, gender identity or expression,
  experience, nationality, appearance, religion, or sexual orientation;
- publish other people's private information without their permission;
- share content that infringes copyright, or ask others to help bypass content protections.

## Scope

This code applies in all project spaces and when someone represents the project in public.

## Reporting

Report unacceptable behaviour to the maintainers through the contact listed in the
repository (see `SECURITY.md` for private contact options). Reports are handled
confidentially. Maintainers may remove comments, commits and other contributions that do
not follow this code, and may temporarily or permanently ban people who repeatedly or
seriously break it.
